## How a rule gets here

- Someone says something about the work: it's recorded (`bin/asf feedback add`) with who said it and what was done.
- If it should hold from now on, it's promoted to a rule above. Said twice is the signal: `bin/asf feedback patterns` lists what keeps coming back.
- A rule that would hold on any site is marked "for the framework too", and proposed there.
- A rule that turns out wrong is struck through with the date and the reason, never silently deleted.
